Planning phase research

The Journey Begins

FarmTech Africa project team

Our project started in the planning phase. Before we could build a smart irrigation system, we first needed to know what data we could actually use - so the project team set out across the University of Pretoria's Hatfield campus in search of data sources.

Looking for sensors and data

We spoke to lecturers and department heads in fields related to our project, looking for sensors we could use for free to collect data. Every conversation helped us understand which data already exists on campus and who looks after it.

Finding the weather station

During this search we came across the Meteorology department, which houses an Automatic Weather Station (AWS) on top of the Geography building. We received permission to view the AWS, and that is where we took our first project team picture.

Weather data is one of the key inputs of a smart irrigation system: knowing whether it is raining, how much rain has fallen and how hot and dry it is helps decide whether the crops need water at all.

This was the first step of our journey. See how all of this data comes together in our FarmTech Africa dashboard.
